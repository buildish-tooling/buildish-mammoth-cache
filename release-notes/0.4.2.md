The Gradle dependency layer is enabled by default. Eligible warm distributed
workers reuse inherited dependency files directly from the seed. Every publisher
in the layered family retains eligible dependency artifacts between runs.

Gradle workflows switching from the ordinary cache family will start cold unless
a previous opt-in has already populated the layered family. Set
`dependency-cache-layer: false` across cooperating jobs to keep the ordinary
cache family and its timestamp GC policy. This also lets workflows with their
own `GRADLE_RO_DEP_CACHE` keep it.

Review `v0.4.2-rc1` as the first release candidate and use `v0.4.2` after final
promotion.
